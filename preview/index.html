Waruna Rathnayaka is a Sri Lankan science educator with a Bachelor of Agriculture and Plantation Management from Wayamba University of Sri Lanka.

His academic background includes plant science, environmental studies, soil science and scientific research. His approach places observation, reasoning and understanding at the heart of science education.
